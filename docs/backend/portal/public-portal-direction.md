# Public Portal Direction

The public Trevvos portal will be rebuilt from zero in the `trevvos-web` Angular repository.

The visual direction should be inspired by the real product system and the KM One Android experience, not by a generic marketing template. The first viewport should make Trevvos and KM One immediately recognizable.

## Initial Goals

- Present Trevvos as the platform.
- Present KM One as the initial primary product.
- Show a catalog of current and future apps.
- Provide a contact page with an interactive LLM-assisted experience.
- Publish rankable SEO article pages.
- Capture leads and waitlist interest.

The backend contracts for the portal live under `/v1/public/*` and should be implemented only when the frontend workflows require them.
