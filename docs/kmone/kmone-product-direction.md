# KM One Product Direction

KM One should understand the driver, vehicle, day and city.

The Android app remains offline-first. It should continue capturing operational data locally and syncing when possible. The backend adds context, personalization, shared intelligence and long-term continuity across devices and future surfaces.

## Product Intelligence

KM One should combine:

- Driver profile and preferences.
- Vehicle profile and costs.
- Day and time context.
- City context.
- Historical usage and feedback.

Florianopolis is the first pilot city for local intelligence. The city context can later include weather, events and operational observations, but collection and scraping are not part of the current foundation.

## Copiloto KM One

The Copiloto should explain recommendations with evidence, origin, validity and confidence. It should guide decision-making without promising demand, rides or earnings.
